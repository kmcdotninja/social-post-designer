# The base family (general layouts)

Simple, sturdy layouts that suit any brand. Fields shared by all: `theme` (primary, paper, tint, accent, ink),
`title` (with one `[key word]`), `caption`; most take `sub`.

| Layout | Composition | Fields | Use it for |
|---|---|---|---|
| `hero` | a sticker, the line huge, one sentence, the call at the foot | kicker, icon, title, sub, cta | launch posts, announcements |
| `statement` | a picture top-left, the line big mid-page, one sentence | art, title, sub | a belief, a promise, a feature |
| `mega` | a short line, then one word or number as wide as the post | title, word, sub | the name, a stat, a single word |
| `countdown` | the number fills the page, "days to go", the line | number, label, title | each day of a countdown |
| `photo` | the photo fills the post, a flat shade on the lower part, the line over it | photo, pos, place, title | image-led posts: places, people, products |
| `frame` | the line on top, the photo in a rounded frame below | photo, pos, title | a product or place shot with a clear line |
| `split` | words on one half, a photo filling the other — **photo only**; without a photo it becomes `corner` | photo, title, sub, cta | a photo that needs half the post |
| `list` | the line, then the services with an icon each (never numbered), the call | items or BRAND.services, title | what we do, how it works |
| `quote` | a giant quotation mark, the saying centred big, who said it | title, by | reviews (real ones only), proverbs |
| `stack` | the line, then words in tilted pills | title, items | what we are, three-word values |
| `quiz` | a big word, the question, lettered answers | word, title, items, note | polls and quizzes |
| `swiss` | one word edge to edge, the line, two columns of detail | word, title, sub, items | bold brand moments |
| `ticket` | the line, a ticket: from → to, three fields, a stub | title, from, fromName, to, toName, fields, stub | journeys, launches, events |
| `tip` | a sticker, the line, a paper card with the tip | kicker, icon, title, body, cta | tips worth saving |
| `corner` | the line top-left, a picture huge running off the bottom-right corner | title, sub, art | features with a picture |
| `places` | the line, then place names as pills | title, items or BRAND.places, note | areas served, engagement |
